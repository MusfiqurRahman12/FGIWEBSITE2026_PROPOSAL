# FGI RFP: Website, Membership Platform & Digital Community Ecosystem

## Metadata
* **Client:** Fashion Group International (FGI) (nonprofit, est. 1930, HQ NYC, fashion/beauty/creative)
* **Websites:** FGI.org | **Social:** @fginyc
* **Deadline:** June 30, 2027
* **Contact:** Maryanne Grisz, President & CEO | Mgrisz@fgi.org (New York, NY)

---

## Core Project Goals
* Modernize FGI brand & digital experience.
* Increase membership acquisition & retention.
* Improve member engagement, networking & communications.
* Streamline membership admin, renewals & reporting.
* Support fundraising, event registration & FGI Student Clubs.
* Ensure scalable, secure, and compliant technology platform.

---

## Website & Front-End Requirements
* **Design:** Visually sophisticated, industry-reflective (fashion/beauty/creative).
* **Technical:** Mobile-responsive, ADA compliant, SEO optimized, analytics-integrated.
* **Content:** Support video integration, media galleries, archives, and stories.
* **Public Pages (Minimum):**
  1. Home
  2. About FGI
  3. Mission & Impact
  4. Leadership & Board
  5. Membership
  6. Events
  7. News & Press
  8. Awards Programs
  9. Scholarships & Grants
  10. Education & Professional Development
  11. Student Programs
  12. Industry Resources
  13. Sponsors & Partners
  14. Contact

### Regional Communities
* **Structure:** Single public-facing directory page for all regional communities (No separate regional sites/systems allowed).
* **Page Features:** Regional names, contacts, social links, geographic location, HQ admin update tools.

---

## Membership Management System
Fully integrated system with the following capabilities:
* **Features:** Online apps, approval workflows, renewals, invoicing, recurring/one-time payment processing, status tracking, history, automated reminders, analytics/reporting.
* **Levels (Customizable Permissions):**
  * Student
  * Emerging Professional
  * Professional
  * Corporate
  * Patron
  * Committee Member
  * Board Member
  * Regional Director
  * Headquarters Administrator
* *Note: HQ Admins must be able to add/modify membership categories.*

---

## Member Portal (Secure, Role-Based Access)
Custom dashboards and content restricted by membership level:
* **Portal Features:** Dashboard, profile management, renewal/payment history, exclusive content, educational resources, recorded programming, industry reports, publications, career resources, volunteer/committee tools, networking.

### Membership Directory
Searchable directory with user privacy controls and member-to-member networking.
* **Search Filters:** Membership category, professional discipline, industry sector, geographic location, FGI region, company, areas of expertise.
* *Note: Admins must be able to add/modify directory filters.*

---

## Events & Registration System
Fully integrated with membership records:
* **Capabilities:** Auto-pricing (member vs. non-member), auto-distinguish guest status, offer membership signup/renewal during registration/checkout, event sponsorship tools, ticket sales, attendee list generation, attendance history tracking, reporting.

---

## Student Club Platform
Initiative at colleges & universities with transitions to professional tiers.
* **Club Admin:** School applications, club registration, faculty advisor & student officer management, approval workflows.
* **Membership:** Individual student & school-affiliated memberships.
* **Engagement:** Targeted communications, resources, mentorship, internships, career tools, networking.
* **Upsell/Transition:** Support smooth transition from student to professional membership post-graduation.

---

## Governance & Leadership Portal
Secure workspace for Regional Directors, Board Members, and HQ Admins.
* **Resources:** Policies & procedures, branding standards, manuals, templates & forms.
* **Tracking & Submissions:** Annual plans, membership/event/financial/board reports, compliance documentation, governance certifications.
* **HQ Admin Dashboard:** Deadline tracking, outstanding submissions, membership/regional metrics, compliance oversight.

---

## Communications & Analytics
* **Targeted Email/Newsletters:** Segmentable by category, region, leadership role, committee participation, and student status.
* **Reporting Dashboards:** Customized views for HQ, Membership Admin, Regional Directors, and Board Leadership.
* **Metrics Tracked:** Growth, retention, renewal rates, event attendance, revenue, student activity, regional participation, sponsorship activity.

---

## Technical & Security Specs
* Cloud-based architecture with secure hosting.
* PCI-compliant payment processing.
* Role-based permissions (RBAC).
* Single Sign-On (SSO) & API integration.
* CRM integration or recommendation.
* Automated backups & security monitoring.
* GDPR and privacy compliance.
* Future-growth scalability.
