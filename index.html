Why Next.js over WordPress / Drupal?

  • Fashion-forward sites demand pixel-perfect design control — template themes cannot deliver this.
  • SSR ensures Google indexes all content while delivering sub-second page loads.
  • React components enable reusable patterns across 14+ public pages and member portals.
  • Automatic image optimization (next/image) serves WebP/AVIF — critical for media-heavy content.

Why not Salesforce?

Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud is $36–$100/user/month, requires certified admins, and has a steeper learning curve. For FGI's scale, HubSpot delivers equivalent functionality at a fraction of the cost.

Cost Notes & Hosting Clarifications

  • All prices are estimates, finalized after the Discovery phase.
  • Hosting & Services ($23,900/yr): These are direct utility and software licensing fees paid to infrastructure providers (AWS, HubSpot, Cloudinary, Sentry, domain registry) to keep the application live and operational during the post-development (post-launch) operational phase. These are standard, pass-through operational expenses and contain no service markup from our agency.
  • Support & Maintenance ($24,000/yr): This covers post-development (post-launch) support, including monthly security updates, backup audits, and 120 support hours per year (10 monthly hours, bankable up to 30 hours) provided by our team.
  • Stripe transaction fees (2.9% + $0.30) are pass-through merchant charges and not included above.
  • HubSpot pricing assumes a 40% nonprofit discount.
